# Regulatory Pathway

 This folder documents the regulatory development pathway for GD2-targeted CAR-T cell therapy in pediatric neuroblastoma.

## Contents

- FDA/EMA Regulatory Checklist — Regulatory planning checklist covering IND submission, FDA expedited programs, EMA requirements, pediatric development, clinical safety, and manufacturing.

## Regulatory Scope

- FDA (United States): IND, Pre-IND meetings, RMAT, Breakthrough Therapy Designation, pediatric requirements, and BLA.

- EMA (European Union): ATMP classification, clinical trial authorization, PRIME, Pediatric Investigation Plan (PIP), and marketing authorization.

  This repository is intended for research and educational purposes. It does not represent a regulatory submission or approval.
